# Miro Browser – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Miro Browser ("Miro", "the app") is a mobile browser with a form-filling feature. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## What the app stores
Miro lets you save one or more profiles containing details you type in, such as names, ages, gender, ID type and ID number, email, mobile number, city, state and PIN code. These profiles are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. You can also save documents (such as ID proof, age proof or photos) in the app. They are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are attached to a website only when you choose to attach them.

## What we collect
We do not collect, transmit or store any of your profile data, browsing history or personal information on our servers. Miro has no account system and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How the app uses your data
Profile data is used only to fill forms on web pages when you tap the Fill or Fill + Continue button. Data is entered into the website you are viewing, and that website's own privacy policy then applies.

## Web search
When you type a search in the address bar, Miro opens the web search page of a third-party search engine (DuckDuckGo) in the app. Your search words are then handled by that search engine under its own privacy policy. Miro does not receive or store your searches on any server.

## Payments and subscriptions
The Fill and Fill + Continue features are part of Miro Pro, a monthly subscription. The subscription is bought and billed entirely by Google Play. Miro never sees or stores your card, bank or UPI details. Miro only asks Google Play whether your account has an active Miro Pro subscription, and keeps the answer (yes or no) on your device. You can cancel any time in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

When a website you visit starts a payment, Miro hands over to the UPI or payment app installed on your phone. Miro never sees or stores those payment details either.

## Third-party websites
Miro is a browser. Websites you visit may collect data under their own policies. We do not control them.

## Data deletion
Delete a profile inside the app, or uninstall the app, and all stored data is removed from your device.

## Children
Miro is not directed at children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Miro stores your profile data only on your device.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The latest version is always at this page.
